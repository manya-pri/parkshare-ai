            PROCESS FLOW ARCHITECTURE
            
The PFD: Frictionless Marketplace Flow

            

              Both sides of the marketplace converge seamlessly at the atomic booking event:
            

            

              
[OWNER] Register Space ➔ Set Hours ➔ AI Estimates Demand ➔ AI Suggests Price ➔ Listed

              
[DRIVER] Search Area ➔ View Recommendations ➔ Select Spot ➔ UPI Escrow Payment

              
[CONVERGE] Booking Confirmed ➔ QR/OTP Gate Check-in ➔ Parking Used ➔ Escrow Released (85%) ➔ Review & AI Retraining
